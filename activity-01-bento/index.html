A Note From Our Florists

Keep your flowers happy

A little care goes a long way. Here's how to enjoy your blooms for longer.

01 — Fresh water

Change the water every day or two.

02 — Trim the stems

Cut stems diagonally before placing them in water.

03 — Find a cool spot

Keep flowers away from direct sunlight and excess heat.

04 — Keep it clean

Remove wilted leaves and petals as needed.
